## Phase 4: Consolidation & Mock Interviews (Weeks 12-14)

### Week 12-13: Mixed Practice

- Pick 2 random problems/day from any completed topic
- Solve under a 25-minute timer
- If you fail, mark it for re-attempt next week

### Week 14: Mock Interviews

- Do 3-4 timed mock interviews (use Pramp, Interviewing.io, or just a timer)
- 1 easy + 1 medium in 45 min
- Practice explaining your thought process out loud
